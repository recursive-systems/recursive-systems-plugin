# Changelog

What changed for owners, newest first. The plugin works through the Recursive Systems connector, which runs on our
servers, so most improvements reach you without a plugin update.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-27

First release.

- Find your heating and cooling company and add it. Nothing is added until you say yes.
- Your report in four parts: Google Maps, Google reviews, Google search and AI answers, each with what changed
  since last month.
- Ask for more detail whenever you want it: Google Maps by time of day, day of the week and distance; each Google
  search by service and town; the questions the AI tools were asked.
- Monitoring (more Google Maps checks at other times of day) and a monthly report, both off until you turn them on.
